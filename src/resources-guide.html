📘 Customer Support Resource Document Formatting Guide

How to prepare documents so the AI can understand them correctly — rewritten for Customer Support interviews, FAQs, and support playbooks. You don’t need technical knowledge. Just follow these rules so answers stay accurate.

📂 1 File Organization (Very Important)

✅ Keep One Topic Per File

Don’t mix unrelated topics in one file. One file should cover one theme or skill area.

❌ BAD (mixed topics)
work history
refund policy
handling angry customers
CRM navigation
shipping delays
product troubleshooting
✅ GOOD (one topic per file)
Work History - Customer Support.docx
Refunds & Returns Playbook.docx
Handling Angry Customers.docx
CRM Notes & Macros.docx
Shipping Delay Response Templates.docx
  • The system chooses the best document first.
  • Mixing topics confuses matching and reduces accuracy.
  • Cleaner files = better answers.
🧠 2 How to Structure Questions & Answers

✅ Use a clear Q then A (with blank line)

✅ Correct Format
What’s your experience in customer support?

I’ve worked in customer support for 3 years supporting email, chat, and phone.
I handled billing questions, order issues, and account access problems.
I used Zendesk and HubSpot to track tickets and follow SLAs.
❌ Do NOT format like this
What’s your experience in customer support? I’ve worked in customer support for 3 years supporting email, chat, and phone...

Rule: Always leave a blank line between question and answer.

🔢 3 Numbered Sections (Highly Recommended)

✅ Number interview Q&A blocks

When writing multiple interview questions in one file, use consistent numbering.

✅ Example
1. Why do you want to work in customer support?

I enjoy solving customer problems and turning negative experiences into positive outcomes.
I like working with clear processes and improving macros/knowledge base articles.

2. How do you prioritize tickets?

I triage by urgency and impact: account lockouts, payment failures, and time-sensitive shipping issues first.
Then I handle standard requests while tracking SLAs and updating customers with ETAs.
  • The system detects numbered blocks.
  • It can return the single most relevant numbered answer.
🪜 4 Step-by-Step Guides

✅ Use “Step 1 / Step 2” for procedures

✅ Example: Refund Process
Step 1: Confirm the customer’s order number and verify purchase date.
Step 2: Check eligibility (return window, condition, exceptions).
Step 3: Offer the best resolution: replacement, store credit, or refund.
Step 4: Process the refund in the billing tool and note the ticket.
Step 5: Send confirmation with the expected refund timeline.
  • The system recognizes “Step 1 / Step 2”.
  • It returns the relevant steps instead of mixing content.
❓ 5 Question Blocks Rule

✅ Keep each question block clean

When using a question format:

  • Question must have a ?
  • Leave a blank line after it
  • Don’t start another question immediately after
✅ Correct
How do you handle an angry customer?

I start by listening without interrupting and acknowledging their frustration.
I restate the issue to confirm I understand and apologize for the experience.
Then I offer clear next steps and timelines, and follow through quickly.
🧩 6 Paragraph Spacing (Very Important)

✅ Separate ideas with blank lines

✅ Good
When customers are upset, empathy comes first.

I acknowledge the impact on them and confirm what outcome they want.

Then I offer the best available resolution and set expectations clearly.
❌ Bad
When customers are upset, empathy comes first. I acknowledge the impact on them and confirm what outcome they want. Then I offer the best available resolution and set expectations clearly.

Blank lines help the system understand context blocks.

🚫 7 Avoid These Mistakes

❌ Don’t do these

  • Mix unrelated topics in one document
  • Put answers on the same line as the question
  • Use inconsistent numbering
  • Paste messy web content with broken formatting
  • Use too many emojis inside the document
  • Rely on scanned PDFs (harder to read)
📝 8 Writing Style Tips

✅ Write for clarity

Use:

  • Clear sentences
  • Short paragraphs
  • Consistent headings
  • Structured lists and steps

Avoid:

  • Giant text walls
  • Random line breaks
  • Overlapping sections
🗂 9 Recommended File Types

✅ Best formats

  • .docx (recommended)
  • .txt

⚠️ Avoid when possible

  • Scanned PDFs (harder to read)
  • Images / screenshots of text
🏆 10 Best Practice Summary

✔ Quick checklist

  • One topic per file
  • Use numbering for interview questions
  • Use “Step 1” format for procedures
  • Leave blank lines between sections
  • Keep formatting clean and consistent
  • Keep documents ~5–10 pages when possible
Simple rule to remember:
If a human can easily scan and understand your document visually… the AI will understand it correctly.
If it looks messy to you… it will be messy for the AI too.
